Player value leaderboard

Rank score = selected production / ln(cap hit in actual dollars). Quarterbacks use a smoothed passing TD/INT ratio instead of raw touchdowns.

Offense assigns passing and rushing stats to QBs, rushing and receiving to RBs, and receiving to WRs/TEs. Defensive rankings also include tackles and sacks. Steelers-only results use the latest roster saved with this run. Defense counts interceptions and opponent-fumble recoveries as takeaways; forced fumbles are shown separately. Cap hits are summed over the selected seasons. Scores are ranked within position group, not across positions. Offensive line and special-team players are omitted because these sources do not provide equivalent individual production statistics for them.
